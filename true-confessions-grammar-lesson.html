Briefing

Detective, you already know the rules — gerund after some verbs, infinitive after others, and a few verbs that need a person in between. Today isn't about learning the rules again. It's about using them without thinking, in real conversation with your teacher.

There are five rooms in this case file. Work through them in order — each one gets a little more talkative than the last.

  1. Sort the Evidence — a quick warm-up to check the patterns are still fresh.
  2. Confession Booth — short personal sentences. Type one, get feedback, move on.
  3. Interrogation Room — speaking cards for you and your teacher.
  4. Courtroom — discuss real cases and give your verdict, out loud.
  5. Case Closed — a two-minute reflection before you leave.
